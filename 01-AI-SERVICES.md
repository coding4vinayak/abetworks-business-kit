## Delivery checklist (run EVERY client through this exact sequence)

**1. Intake (Day 0)** — collect in ONE message:
- [ ] Business type + top 10–20 customer questions (or their FAQ page/photo of price list)
- [ ] Website/IG link, logo, agreed bot name
- [ ] Where leads should land (email / Google Sheet — build the sheet FOR them)
- [ ] 2 answers you must get exactly right (e.g. exact opening hours)

**2. Build (Day 1–2)** — Botpress: persona = "polite staff member of [Business]", knowledge = their info verbatim, fallback = capture contact, never improvise. Test 20 questions incl. 5 nasty ones ("why are you so expensive", "do you deliver to [other city]", wrong-language test).

**3. Demo video (Day 2)** — 2-min Loom: their name in the first sentence → 3 real Q&As → lead landing in the sheet. This video is your upsell + your next portfolio piece.

**4. Publish (Day 3–4)** — embed code sent as a 1-min Loom "how I added it" OR paste-it-for-them via screen-share. Test on THEIR phone browser (most owners only look on mobile).

**5. Handover (Day 5)** — delivery pack: 1-page PDF (what it does, how to edit Q&As if you bought Standard+, your support contact) + ask the referral line (script #9) + ask for review on the platform + offer Care plan with 1-month free first.

**Time per client at this point of maturity: 4–7 hrs total.** This number is your profit. Above 10 hrs = you're re-customizing too much → back to templates.

## Retainer playbook ($20–50/mo = your passive floor)

What "Care plan" actually includes (define it so delivery stays under 2 hrs/mo/client):
- 1 update/refresh of Q&As per month (10 min)
- Lead sheet sanity check + forward stragglers (10 min)
- 1 mini report: "Your bot answered X chats, captured Y leads this month" (15 min — screenshot of Botpress analytics, huge perceived value)
- Bot stays online (free tier) — you handle if limits hit

**Convert the maintenance offer at delivery, not later:** "First month free — then it's $30/mo. Cancel anytime." 60–70% stay. 10 clients × $35 = $350/mo for ~1 day of work/month.

## Zero-hallucination policy (your #1 trust differentiator — put it in every proposal)

1. Bot answers ONLY from client-provided knowledge base.
2. Questions outside it → never guessed. It says: "I'll have the team contact you" + captures name/phone.
3. You test 20 questions incl. 5 adversarial ones before every delivery.
4. You show the client this test live in the demo video — instant trust, nobody else does this.

## Scaling ladder (don't skip steps)

1. **You do everything** (month 1–2) — learn delivery patterns, build templates for clinic/restaurant/shop/demo script.
2. **Templates phase** (month 2–3) — every niche you serve twice gets a reusable bot template. New client = clone + fill = 6 hrs → 2 hrs.
3. **Subcontract phase** (month 4+, only when you have 3+ months of income saved) — hire a junior (job boards, student groups, local Telegram/Discord dev groups) at 30% of project value for build+test; you keep sell + demo + QA. Your hours drop, capacity doubles.
4. **Agency framing** (month 6+) — same service, brand name, 2–3 subcontractors, you sell full-time. Price premiums of 30–50% over your solo rates for "team + SLA."

Rule: if you can't sell 5 of something yourself, you can't automate delivering it.

# 10 — SCALE TO AGENCY (Month 6+ file — don't open the lid earlier)

**Trigger condition to start this file:** 3 consecutive months averaging $800+/mo AND client-delivery eats >50% of your week hours. Not before. Premature hiring = paying strangers to watch you.

---

## Why hire (the only right reason)

You're not buying "help." You're buying **hours per week** to sell demos, build retainers, and make content. Delivery is the $/hr-cheapest thing you do — so it's the FIRST thing delegated, always.

## The delegation ladder (in order, never skip)

| Level | Task | At income | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | (you) outreach, demos, pricing, remaining QA | always | your crown jewels — never delegated |
| 1 | Lead-sheet checking, forwarding, monthly report screenshots | $600/mo | 5 hrs/wk × cheap VA |
| 2 | Bot builds FROM YOUR TEMPLATES (clinic/restaurant/shop) | $800/mo | near-copy builds; they screen-record the demo, you review before sending |
| 3 | CAD bolt-standard conversions, render jobs | $1,200/mo | student engineers 3–5$/hr |
| 4 | Client intake form processing | $2,000/mo | only after L1–3 stable 60 days |
| 5 | Raw content editing | $2,000/mo + | Shorts editing is a commodity skill |

## Finding builders (South Asia specific)

- Local university engineering/CS student groups (Telegram/WhatsApp/FB) — juniors hungry for $3–6/hr and a real portfolio
- "Hire" posts on university job boards / co-working posters
- Your own DMs: message people who ran decent small gigs on Fiverr and are stuck at zero reviews
- Never hire from guru-course audiences (they'll know less than you and overthink)

## The $5 test-task hiring filter (run everybody through it)

1. Send a real piece: "Here's a sketch photo → deliver a STEP by [date]" or "Here's the clinic template → build this demo bot + 1-min screen capture"
2. Pay them for their time ($5). Serious people agree fast; flakes vanish — which is the filter working.
3. Judge ONLY: deadline · following instructions · communication clarity · one question max
4. One week trial-batch at 3–5$/hr part-time (max 2 workers at once; more chaos than help before that unless reviews demand)

## SOP template (give each task exactly once, then it's theirs)

```
TASK: [e.g., "Build clinic demo bot"]
GOAL: what "done" looks like (1 sentence + example)
STEPS: numbered, with screenshots or a Loom of YOU doing it
TOOLS: links/accounts
RULES: what NEVER to do (never improvise answers, never message clients without master review)
DUE: cadence
CHECKLIST: 5 checkboxes for the last pass
```
Record yourself once doing the task with commentary = SOP 90% done. Store in a shared drive folder "SOPs"; update every time something goes wrong.

## Margin math (your new scoreboard)

- Charge $120 bot build → pay builder $35 + VA overhead $5 → **you keep $80 for selling + QA** (≈2 hrs of your time = $40/hr passive-ish)
- Retainer $35/mo → builders touch only if update exceeds template (rare) → ~85% margin
- If a delivered project's margin < 50% → either re-template it or stop selling it. Margin when your hands are off is the only real income.

## Rules that keep the train on rails

- **Client is ALWAYS yours.** Your subcontractors never meet clients, never share contact, never complain "payment split" to clients. Sign a 1-page NDA-lite + "no side dealing" clause (Template A4 addendum).
- **QA is you:** spot-check every delivery: 20-question test incl. 5 adversarial; CAD models: check mass/units dims vs sketch — 15 minutes. Repeat a builder's name in a happy client testimonial? give them the bonus, they're keepers.
- **Firing rule, written down:** 2 deadline misses or 1 quality miss with no fix → out, no debate. Kind sentence, fast execution: "This isn't a fit, I'll pay for the completed portion, glad to refer you elsewhere."
- **Name an agency brand only at ~$1,500/mo** (a simple wordmark on the dashboard + email signature is enough; no office, never an office).

## Phase map (where this ends up)

| Phase | You do | They do | Monthly income shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6–9 mo | sell, demo, QA | L1–2 | $1,500–2,500 (60% services + retainers) |
| 9–15 mo | sell, partnerships, content | L3–4 + editor | $2,500–5,000 |
| 15+ mo | sell + acquire (1 funnel: content in) | delivery team, VA team | $5k+ with 10–15 hrs delivery/week |

That's the "passive but operated" state you asked for: **the machine works daily, but it's YOUR machine.**

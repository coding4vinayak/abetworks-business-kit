## B. CLIENT DASHBOARD CANVAS ("Your AI Front Desk" — every delivery)

**Deliver in ONE tidy package on handover day. 30 min in Google Sites + Sheets. Creates wow + soft-locks the retainer.**

### Header
**[Business Name] — AI Front Desk** · Live since [date] · Status: 🟢 Active
[METRICS] This month: **X chats · Y leads captured · Z hours saved**
*(X/Y from Botpress analytics; Z = chats × ~3 min × local labor cost — honest "estimate, not invoice" framing.)*

### Section 1 — "Your assistant, live"
Embedded chat window (iframe) so owners refresh and see their bot — instant demo machine.

### Section 2 — "Leads captured"
Google Sheet: timestamp · name · phone · question asked · status (New/Contacted/Won/Lost).
Owner instructions visible right there: "Reply within 15 min while lead is warm. Data stays yours — you are the owner of the sheet."

### Section 3 — "What your assistant knows"
The Q&A list from their intake form. Line at top: "Send me any change and it's updated within 24h — that's what the Care plan is for."

### Section 4 — "This month's report"
Copy-paste template you reuse monthly:
> **AI Front Desk — Monthly Snapshot**
> 💬 Chats handled: X · 📥 Leads captured: Y · ⏱ Time saved (est.): Z hours
> 3 real conversations:
> 1. [Q] → [A] (captured a lead on [date])
> 2. [Q] → [A]
> 3. [Q] → [A]
> Next month suggestion: add [promotions/booking flow/2nd language] — included in Care plan.

*(3 highlights, never full logs — powerful, readable, 15 min.)*

### Section 5 — "Upgrades that make money"
1 line each: "Leave/no-show reminders [niche] upgrade — $X one-time · Available this week."
*(Never static. A dashboard that shows growth stops "cancel in month 2.")*

### The monthly re-engagement email (retention engine)

> **Subject:** [Business] AI Front Desk — October report 👇
>
> Hi [Name], your assistant had a busy month 👇
> 💬 X customer chats · 📥 Y new leads in your sheet

> One thing I'd add next month: [1 suggestion tied to THEIR business]

> [Your name] · [dashboard link]

Send from the MONTHLY report section. Clickable link to dashboard. "October" doesn't need to be perfect — just exists. This one email is why retainers don't cancel.

## C. Client-side intake form (before every build — paste into WhatsApp/Upwork)

> Quick 5-min form so I get your bot right the first time:
> 1. Business type + location/city?
> 2. What are the top 10–20 questions customers ask? (list, photo, or link)
> 3. Anything the bot must NEVER say (discounts, refunds, owner's personal number)?
> 4. Exact answers for 2 questions (e.g. hours, delivery area — must be perfect)
> 5. Where leads should land: email or Google Sheet?
> 6. Language(s) customers use most?
> 7. Anything the bot should push (new offer, booking link)?

## D. Copy-paste message scripts

### D1. Value-first (Month 2+ — for slightly warmer leads: QR/card/label idea)
> Hi [Name] — quick idea for [Business]: you have a QR code / card / label on the counter. What if scanning it opened an AI assistant that answers menu/price questions instantly AND lets customers table-book/ask about availability — 24/7 — even when your counter is closed? I set these up in 5 days, done-for-you. Ever tried one? Happy to show a 2-min demo of it on YOUR info.

### D2. Warm body version — warm referral intro
> Hi [Name], [Mutual contact] suggested I reach out — they mentioned you're handling customer questions manually on [channel]. I build AI front desks that answer 24/7 and capture every serious customer's name + number. Want a 2-min demo based on your business info? No obligation.

### D3. E-calendar grid / availability ask (restaurant/clinic close)
> Last thing: most [restaurants/clinics] ask the bot for [table availability / appointments]. Should it show real-time slots or collect name/phone/time and forward to you? (Real-time needs your calendar refresh — add-on price: $X.)

**Availability addon:** Google Calendar integration or a weekly sheet the owner edits 1× week (simpler → include in Standard).

### D4. Excel/data→bot conversion lead-in ("which we haven't yet")
> Hi [Name] — do you run your price lists, quotations, or inventory on Excel? I turn those into an AI assistant: your staff/customer types "price for 100 units?" and gets an instant, correct, in-policy answer. Data stays in your file — bot reads it, nothing changes about how you store it. 2-min demo on YOUR sheet? (Free, no obligation.)
- **You will hear "data privacy" objections here** (manufacturers, trading firms) → answer: "Data stays in YOUR Google account — the assistant only reads it; nothing is stored on my side, nothing is public." Files stay in the client's Google Drive.

### D5. Beta test group message (day 21 — GP / WhatsApp groups / your UPS' own groups)
> Hi everyone — I'm building AI chat assistants for local businesses (restaurants, clinics, shops) that answer customer questions 24/7 and collect every serious buyer's contact. I'm onboarding 5 beta clients this month at a reduced one-time price in exchange for feedback + a review if satisfied. If you or someone you know might want this — DM me, I'll show a 2-min demo of their business. (Group members: ignore if not relevant 🙂)

### D5-alt. Personal ask (1:1 variant of D5)
> Hi [Name] — I'm launching AI chat assistant setups for local businesses (24/7 answers + lead capture). I'm onboarding the first 5 clients at a reduced one-time price for feedback + review. Anyone come to mind — you, or someone you know? Happy to show a free 2-min demo first.

### D6. Demo follow-up script (send 24h after any demo video)
> Hi [Name], did you get a chance to watch the demo (2 min)? Quick Q so I know if this is useful: was there a question customers ask that you DIDN'T see answered in the demo? Tell me and I'll show you that exact case — takes 1 minute.

**Why D6 works:** it never asks "did you like it" (unanswerable), it asks for ONE missing question — that reply IS a yes to customize, and the next video basically closes.

## G. Danger zone (skim once, remember the rules)

- **Golden rule:** sell one thing at one price per conversation. Options kill momentum (never send 3 options to a non-expert owner).
- Family/friends paying you ₹/$ zero? Free setups for 2 people MAX, and they owe you a detailed testimonial + 2 referrals.
- Never DM-spam more than the 4-message cadence (0/1/3/7) — reputation is compounding capital; don't spend it.
- Never DM-spam a group (D5 once, then 1:1 only).
- Public groups: value comments first, DM only if they engage or ask.
- Never leave anyone worse off than you found them — screenshots travel.

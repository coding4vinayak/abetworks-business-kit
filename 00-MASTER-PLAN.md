## Weekly schedule (use the 25-hr version until income > $500/mo, then 35–40)

### 25-hr version

| Day | Block 1 (2h) | Block 2 (1–2h) |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Outreach 20 msgs + follow-ups | Botpress/CAD client work |
| Tue | Client work / build bots | Digital product build (1 product, split across 3 weeks) |
| Wed | Upwork proposals (5) + replies | Client work |
| Thu | Outreach 20 msgs + follow-ups | Digital product build |
| Fri | Client work | Record Loom/demo, update portfolios |
| Sat | Deliveries + collections + ask every client for referral | Weekly review checklist (below) |
| Sun | OFF (protect it — burnout kills month 3) | — |

### Rule of thumb per week
- 60% client delivery · 25% sales/outreach · 15% passive asset building. **Never 0 on outreach** — even in busy weeks, that's the #1 mistake.

## Weekly review checklist (every Saturday, 20 min, one Google Sheet tab)

- [ ] Outreach messages sent this week? (target 100) Replies? Demos given? Deals won?
- [ ] Every delivered client → asked for review + 2 referrals?
- [ ] Retainers: who is close to converting maintenance offer?
- [ ] Product work: which week of the 3-week product cycle am I in? On track?
- [ ] Trackers updated (outreach sheet + income sheet)?
- [ ] ONE thing to drop or fix next week. Write it down.

## Pivot/kill rules (write these somewhere you see them)

1. A service type with **0 closes after 20 real offers** → change the offer or the channel, not the business.
2. A Fiverr gig with **0 orders after 60 days + 1,000 impressions** → rewrite thumbnail + title before touching description.
3. Any routine task you've done **5+ times** → template it or script it with AI. Templates are your unpaid employees.
4. Do not start Business 4 (agency/courses) before Month 6, no matter how hyped you feel.

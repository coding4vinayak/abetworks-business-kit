**Role:** your future "sales team that works while you sleep." Starts month 2–3, 3 hrs/week max. Content here is NOT for ad money — it's a lead-generation and product-selling machine.

---

## Platform + niche decision (pick ONE, commit 90 days)

- **Primary: YouTube** (long videos 5–8 min + Shorts cuts). It compounds forever and feeds searches.
- Positioning (your unfair angle): **"Mechanical engineer using AI"** — a human story + two audiences (engineers + business owners).
- Language: English OR your local language — pick by audience. Local language = faster local clients. English = global products income. Choose your money goal.

## 3 content pillars (rotate in order)

1. **Build-in-public** (40%) — "I built this restaurant's AI assistant in 4 days" — anonymized, show the bot working. This sells services 1:1. Best performers.
2. **Tutorials / problems** (40%) — "Why 3D prints snap — the wall-thickness rule", "5 customer questions your DMs lose every night", "2D drawing to 3D model in 20 min". These build the audience that buys products later.
3. **Offers / proof** (20%) — case-study recaps, "here's what one $150 setup did for a clinic", product announcements. Convert viewers into buyers.

## Video script skeleton (7 min long-form)

1. **HOOK (0:00–0:10):** show the END result first (bot answering live, finished render) + "I'll show you how, in less than 7 minutes."
2. **STAKES (0:10–0:30):** why this costs money / what the pain is — one story.
3. **BUILD (0:30–5:00):** screen-record, cut all dead time, speed 2x where fine. Stamp labels: "Step 1 — knowledge base".
4. **PROOF (5:00–6:00):** real result — numbers, before/after.
5. **CTA (6:00–6:30):** ONE action only — "free template in description (Gumroad)" OR "DM me 'BOT' for a free demo." Never 2 CTAs.

**Shorts:** cut the 40 best seconds of every long video. 1 long = 3 Shorts minimum. Post 3–5 Shorts/week.
**Titles:** use the exact words your audience types ("3d printing", "2d to 3d", "chatbot for business"). Titles > thumbnails → say the whole promise in words.

## Repurposing map (1 piece of content = 6 assets)

1 Long YouTube → 3 Shorts → 1 LinkedIn post (text recap) → 1 Pinterest pin (products) → 1 Fiverr gig image idea → 1 email in client funnel (later).

## Rules that prevent quitting

- First 40 videos or 12 months — whichever first — you just don't quit. Judged after 40, churn after 3 is the #1 killer.
- Screen-recording your CLIENT work is content. You're already working — just hit record (get permission to remove client name; or rebuild a demo version).
- Batch: 1 filming day (2 hrs) per week gives all Shorts. Editing while you eat dinner, whatever it takes — 3 hrs cap.
- Don't buy gear: phone + free CapCut/Shotcut + $2 phone tripod (or books). Upgrade only once a month of content pays for it 10×.

# BUSINESS 3 — Digital Products (Passive Layer)

**Role:** earns while you sleep. Built from things you ALREADY make/provide for clients. Budget: 2 hrs/week max. Do NOT expect real money before month 3–4 — this is the long game.

---

## Golden rule

**Never build a product you haven't already done for a client.** Reuse = profit. From scratch = trap. Every client deliverable → ask "can a version of this be sold 50 times?"

---

---

## Etsy digital downloads — setup notes

- Listing: 5–8 preview images (Canva). First image = bold overview of what's inside, not a pretty cover.
- Immediately after sale → buyer can rate you. Bundle a thank-you note in delivery: "Reply with issue + I fix it in 24h."
- Etsy fee: $0.20/listing + ~6.5% + payment fees. Price accordingly.
- Promote inside your gigs' niche: "as used in my client projects" — link the product from your Fiverr bio and video descriptions (never spam links in client chats).

## Gumroad — why it matters

Instant checkout, no listing fees. Use as the link you paste in YouTube descriptions, your LinkedIn posts, and in client's final deliverables ("Free 10 Q&A checklist included: [link]"). Delivery doubles as marketing. And always capture the client's country details — Gumroad handles VAT.

---

## Marketing engine for products (only 2 hrs/wk)

- **Pinterest + Etsy's own search** (women-free: mechanical drawings and calculators ranked via Pinterest are strongly visual) → schedule 3 pins/product with Canva, keyword title + description.
- **YouTube Shorts engine light version** (later, month 4+): 30s "watch me turn a sketch into a print-ready part" → end-card pointing to product kit.

**Week-3 targeting warning:** your first product's first 30 days WILL earn almost nothing. This is normal. Judge it at day 90, not day 7.

The questions you'd ask anyway

If yours isn't here, the trust centre probably has it — or just ask.

Will it make up prices or services? (hallucination)

No — it answers only from your approved information. Anything outside it, it collects the customer's contact instead of guessing. We demonstratively retest this monthly: 20 questions, five deliberately nasty.

Do I need WhatsApp Business API?

To start, no — webchat goes live on day 5. WhatsApp needs Meta business approval and Meta-side invoicing; we handle the setup as a fixed-price add-on and explain Meta's pricing honestly before you commit.

Who owns the data and leads?

You do. Your sheet, your CRM, exportable any time. Data residency in Mumbai (ap-south-1), DPDP-ready handling — the same trust layer our enterprise clients pass audits on.

My staff aren't technical. Is that a problem?

None. The monthly report is one page. The update path is a single WhatsApp message to us. Training is a 15-minute video your team keeps.

Can I cancel?

The care plan: yes, 7 days' notice — retainers are optional by design. Setup: after go-live it stands, since it paid for the delivered system, knowledge base and training.

Is this for big companies?

No — deliberately. The enterprise lane lives on our main services page. Allied services is small-business shaped: same trust, tighter budget.

## Portfolio (Days 2–5, before listing gigs)

1. **Photo-to-model:** take a photo of any bolted object around you (a wall-mount fan bracket is perfect) → model it → side-by-side image "photo vs model." Best-performing single image in this niche.
2. **Amazon-style product render:** model a simple consumer product (water bottle lid, phone stand) + 3 studio renders.
3. **Manufacturing drawing:** turn model #1 into a full GD&T drawing sheet w/ title block.

The photo-vs-model and before/after pattern repeats everywhere: **proof, not promises, sells design work.**

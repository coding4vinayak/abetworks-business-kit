# 09 — MONEY SYSTEM (how the money actually behaves once it arrives)

**Role:** stops you from being the freelancer who earns $1,200/month and still can't buy a laptop. Financial rules start the day FIRST dollar lands.

---

## The 3-jar system (3 accounts, or 3 mobile-wallet pockets — works fine)

| Jar | Share | Purpose | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SURVIVE** | 50% | bills, food, transport | your only spending jar |
| **MOAT** | 30% | emergency fund buffer | target: 3 months of your costs, later 6. Untouchable except true emergencies |
| **GROWTH** | 20% | tools, ads later, subcontractors, courses later | only spent via the ladder below |

Withdraw from Payoneer every 2 weeks (balance timing) if you can, rather than daily — keeps FX decisions calm, not emotional.

## The reinvestment ladder (buy in THIS order, never out of order)

| At income of | Buy | Why now |
|---|---|---|
| Any | $2 phone trip bracket / sticky note stand | better demo videos, better gigs |
| $100/mo stable | A custom-domain upgrade later? NO → keep in Moat | patience beats branding |
| $300/mo | Loom Pro or paid bot tier (only if 5+ clients need it) | client volume demands it |
| $500/mo | Canva Pro (once $2,000+ of gigs ran on it) | nicer deliverables, batch production |
| $800/mo | Your own domain + Google Sites + email ([yourname]@) | inbound clients, content CTA links |
| $1,200/mo | **First subcontractor** (see 10-SCALE-AGENCY) | this is when buying hours pays |
| $2,000+/mo | Mic + light (content lane) | content now worth better quality |

## Golden rules

1. **Tools must return 10×** what they cost monthly, else wait. Canva $13 = needs $130 of earned extra output per month. Numbers, not vibes.
2. **No ads before month 6, no matter what.** Ads amplify a machine that converts — with zero reviews it just burns. Your ads substitute is the outreach engine.
3. **Never buy "play" for motivation** — the gurus' gear, courses, crypto. Only tools in the ladder.
4. **Client prepayments are not your money yet** — they're "work tokens." They spend on delivery only.
5. **The "first $10k" rule:** your first $2–3k of profit buys risk-free base (Moat), the SECOND buys freedom of choice — until then, you're under construction, not rich, and that's fine.

## Runway & the quit-the-job/quit-college question

- **The 1.5× rule:** don't quit your salary source until your business net averaged 1.5× your salary for **3 consecutive months** AND Moat ≥ 3 months costs. Before that: duplicate — mornings engine, evening job/studies.
- If you're a student: business hours come from the 25-hr plan; your degree = CAD lane credibility anyway.
- If family pushes "get a real job": show the Income tracker (05) monthly — numbers argue better than you do.

## South Asia practical layer (non-advice, habits only)

- **Keep a separate EVIDENCE folder:** every invoice/screenshot/platform payout in one drive folder, monthly subfolders. When tax/loan/visa paperwork comes, you just hand it over. From day 1.
- **Register when required by your country's rules** — typically when income crosses the local threshold; consult one local accountant ONCE when you reach ~$500/mo (usually $20–50 of value). Don't DIY law, don't panic either.
- **Invoice in USD**, withdraw when the FX chart is friendly for your local currency — a 2–3% difference on $1,000 meals for a week.
- **Family expectations** ("you earn now, help at home"): pre-decide a fixed amount (e.g. 20–30% of SURVIVE jar if you share a household) and a fixed date. Generosity with rules > resentment in silence.

## The one number to watch weekly

**Months of runway** = MOAT jar ÷ your monthly survival costs. Goal line: never let it fall under 2. It's the number that lets you refuse bad clients and keep prices high. Protect it before you protect your pride.

# 11 — LEARNING LADDER (what to learn, when, and what to deliberately skip)

**Rule:** learn on-demand, pulled by a paying problem — never "for later." Every hour of learning must map to a client outcome or a product. This file = your curriculum so YouTube rabbit-holes don't eat your weeks.

---

## The 90-day curriculum (30 min/day, attached to the daily engine)

### Weeks 1–2 — Bot fundamentals (serves B1 immediately)
- Botpress official docs + their YouTube channel: knowledge base, workflows, capture flows, fallback
- Deliverable check: rebuild the restaurant demo TWICE, second time in under 60 min
- Mini-drill: 20 questions incl. 5 nasty ones ("do you deliver to [other city]?", "why are you expensive?") — bot must gracefully fail-forward

### Weeks 3–4 — Prompting for business bots (serves the zero-hallucination brand)
- System-prompt patterns: persona + "answer ONLY from provided info" + escalation phrasing
- One-pager drill: rewrite 10 local-business Q&As into clean bot-ready answers
- Resource: OpenAI/Antropic free prompting guides ONLY (no $400 courses — the ladder says no)

### Weeks 5–6 — Selling (your sales skill upgrade — the highest-$/hr skill you own)
- Drill the 04 scripts OUT LOUD, local language, 10 runs each until natural
- The 10 English sales phrases to master (for Upwork calls):
  1. "Just so I understand correctly, you want..." (recap = trust)
  2. "Here's what I'd suggest, and here's why..."
  3. "That's outside the scope, but I can add it for $X."
  4. "Fair point. My honest answer is..."
  5. "What would need to be true for you to say yes this week?"
  6. "I'd rather lose a project, than take one I cannot deliver well."
  7. "The first milestone would be..."
  8. "I've built exactly this before — let me show it."
  9. "That's a good question — I've never tested that. Let me check and answer you by tomorrow." (honest > instant)
  10. "Does Wednesday 5pm work for a 15-min call?"
- Watch 3 Upwork winning-proposal breakdowns; steal structure, never copy words

### Weeks 7–8 — CAD polish for gigs (serves B2)
- GD&T quick refresh (position/flatness/runout only — you have the degree; it's engine oiling)
- DFM for 3D printing: orientation, trapped volumes, clearance fits, shrinkage handling
- Renders portfolio-grade in Blender/Fusion: 3 lighting setups, transparent background PNG
- Deliverable check: photo-to-model job done in < 2.5 hrs at sellable quality

### Weeks 9–12 — Systems + content (serves scale)
- Build the full SOP for clinic/restaurant/shop bot delivery (10-SCALE: Loom yourself once)
- Notion/Google-Docs template polish: your intake form, delivery pack, dashboard site
- Content engine start (07) — batch your first 2 videos + 6 Shorts from client work and demos

## The "teach it" multiplier (never skip)

Every time you learn anything: it must LEAVE as either (a) a template, (b) a Short/YouTube piece, or (c) a gig/portfolio image. Knowledge that doesn't produce an asset in 7 days gets relearned by someone else and sold to your clients. Harsh rule, big edge.

## Do NOT learn this year (write on your wall)

- ❌ SaaS/MVP coding ("no CSIT" — correct strategy, not an apology)
- ❌ Crypto/trading/ML theory (unless a client need pulls it mid-project)
- ❌ Another platform (Shopify/tiktok/ads-manager mechanics) before month 6
- ❌ Any paid course until it maps to a specific hire/tools decision at a specific income level (09 ladder)
- ✅ Instead: deep mastery of 3 tools max (Botpress + Fusion 360/Onshape + Canva). Shallow broad = broke. Deep narrow = booked.

## Weekly learning loop (in the Saturday review)

1. What client problem did I hit this week that I fumbled? → next week's learning slot = ONLY that
2. Which template/asset did I produce from new knowledge? (name it or it didn't happen)
3. Any tool I almost bought? → check ladder in 09 before it's allowed

If a week passes with no learning slot used, fine — but you must still answer #1. Learning on-demand only works if the demand question is asked.

## Discipline rules for all trackers

1. Update trackers DAILY (5 min, part of the 60–75 min engine block) — never "on weekends."
2. One sheet file per everything (3 tabs) — separate files die of neglect.
3. If a status hasn't changed in 14 days → message on day 7 rule #4, then archive to "Lost."
4. Never lie in it. The sheet is your manager, not your audience.

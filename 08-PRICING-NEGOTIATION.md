# 08 — PRICING & NEGOTIATION BIBLE (what to say when they push back)

**Role:** converts your scripts into income. The difference between a $50 client and a $300 client is often 3 sentences.

---

## The 5 non-negotiable pricing principles

1. **Never negotiate price — negotiate scope.** The price is the price; the QUESTION is what's included. Say this exact sentence when they push:
   > "The price reflects what's included. Let me match what's included to your budget instead — tell me what you'd use most, and I'll build that version."
2. **Lead with the middle option.** Present Standard first, Basic only when they hesitate, Premium only when they ask "what's the best you do?" 65% of clients pick what's put in front of them.
3. **Anchor before you quote.** Never say a number cold: "A setup like this typically runs $250–400 for foreign clients — for a local business like yours, my rate is $150. That's why I asked about your scope first."
4. ** Discounts exist for 3 reasons only:** first client in a niche, a written review & a case study swap, or a stepping-stone scope (smaller). NEVER discount because it's "the end of the month."
5. **50% upfront,** always, client #4 onward. Frame it without apologizing: "I start 50/50, second half on delivery — that protects both of us."

## The objection script bank

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive" / "best price?" | Scope match line (#1 above) → offer a SMALLER scope at a REAL price. Never a discount on the same scope. |
| "Can you give it for cheaper, we'll refer you a lot of people" | "I'll offer a referral BONUS instead: for every client you send, you get one month of Care plan free. The setup price stays as is — otherwise I'm under-serving you." |
| "The other guy quoted $X, way less" | "That might be the right choice for you — quality chatbots differ in the details: hallucination control, lead capture, hands-on testing. I do that testing on camera with you. If those things matter, I'm your option." |
| "We'll think about it" | "Sure — most people think with more information. Is it the price, the trust, or the timing? I can solve each separately." (Name their true objection FOR them.) |
| "Can we start next month?" | "Absolutely — I'll send your plan + my calendar link. Build slots go fast though. Should I hold one for you next week?" |
| "Do it free, then we'll see" | "Free doesn't work for my business — but I have a $40 starter version: top-10 questions only, 1 week, you see results. That gives me a chance to earn the big setup." |
| "You're new / young" | "Fair doubt — that's exactly why I demo on YOUR business info before you pay and include 30-day support. You risk nothing but 5 minutes of watching." |
| "AI is risky / will lie to my customers" | The zero-hallucination policy, rehearsed (01-AI-SERVICES). Show the cut-off behavior live. |

## Price raise ladder (do not skip, do not delay)

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| Every 5 platform reviews | +$10–30 on both Basic and Standard |
| Every 3 retainer clients | +$5/mo on NEW Care plans only (grandfather existing) |
| Calendar full for 2 consecutive weeks | +30% across the board |
| First inbound client who never negotiated | +$50 setup on new clients, keep them if they say yes |

**Never raise on an active client. Power move stays: raise, then honor old price for 60 days if they ask ("loyal client mouth" policy).**

## Scope creep control (kills margins — use the table)

- In every SOW, a mini table: **Included** | **Extra ($X each)**. Typical extras: additional language +$30 · more than 30 Q&As +$20 · WhatsApp channel +$50 · 2 more revisions +$15.
- When they ask "can it also—": **"Yes — that's an extra $X. Want me to add it?"** Then stop talking. This one line makes scope creep PROFITABLE.
- Revisions cap: 2 included (Premium: 5). Frame: "2 full review rounds included; that covers what most clients need; extra round is $15."
- Cancellation: if client cancels mid-build, 30% of setup fee is due for work done. In your SOW (Template A4).

## The 3 soft closes

1. **Assumptive close** (best for local): "So I'll add table-by-booking for weekends too, leads going to your email — I start tomorrow, you'll see the demo video Tuesday. Sound right?"
2. **Micro-commitment:** "Let's start with the demo — if the demo wins you over, we talk price." (Zero-risk yes. Demo is 10 min of your time.)
3. **Urgency only if true:** "I have 2 build slots next week, and then I'm booked 10 days." Use ONLY when true. Fake urgency in a small market = screenshots that kill you dead.

## Walk-away red flags (fire the prospect BEFORE you fire the client)

- Wants free work "to see how serious you are" after seeing the demo
- Talks down to you; wants to pressure you on WhatsApp at 1am — that's the 24/7 you get
- "Other freelancers charge me less but deliver worse, you know how it is"
- No clarity on who approves the project ("my partner will confirm later" ×3 times)

Politely exit line:
> "I don't think this is the right match right now — happy to reconsider if circumstances change. Best of luck with the project!"

**Rule of compounding:** sales skills make each conversation 2× more likely to close — but they compound ONLY if the outreach engine (04) keeps the pipeline full daily.

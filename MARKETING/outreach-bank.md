## 1. WhatsApp cold messages (20/day, manual, one at a time)

### W-1 opener (default)
---
Hi [+name]! I work with [dentists/restaurants/shops] in [+area]. Quick question — who handles your enquiries after 7 pm?

If missed calls/repeat "price?" texts are a thing, I built something to see: our AI front desk answers questions ONLY from the owner's own price list — try to make it invent a discount, it won't: [DEMO LINK]

15-min free demo setup if you like it. No pitch otherwise, promise.
---

### W-2 opener (demo-challenge, most fun — use 50% of the time)
---
Challenge for you [+name] — open this and TRY to make the demo bot invent a price or a discount (it answers from a signed document only): [DEMO LINK]

We install the same brain for businesses like [+their type] — live in 5 working days. Risk: zero, you don't even pay until you see YOUR demo.
---

### W-3 follow-up (day 4, reply to thread)
---
Closing the loop [+name] — week 1 numbers from a live client: [x] chats handled, [x] leads captured, 0 staff hours. Worth 15 minutes this week or should I check back next month?
---

### W-4 breakup (day 10)
---
Last one from me [+name] — I stop reaching out now. If reception/enquiry chaos bites you again, the demo link works anytime: [DEMO LINK]. All the best either way 🙏
---

## 2. Cold email (10/day) — subject bank

1. After-hours enquiries at [+business]? (not a salesy email)
2. I tried to make this bot invent a price (you'll enjoy this)
3. Missed calls = lost patients/customers (+fix inside)
4. Your reception between 7pm–11pm
5. A receptionist that can't lie (20-question proof)
6. [+their name] — 412 chats, 0 staff hours (real week-1 numbers)

### Email body (use with subject 2/5 — the proof-first body)
---
Hi [+name],

One honest test before anything else: open [DEMO LINK] and try to make the demo bot invent a price or a discount. You can't. It answers only from a signed knowledge document, and every answer is checked by a 20-question adversarial retest before it ever faces a customer.

Businesses like yours lose most enquiries between 7pm and 11pm — WhatsApp, missed calls, staff gone home. We install an AI front desk that answers those, captures numbers as leads, and hands anything unusual to a human instead of guessing.

Live in 5 working days. Week-1 proof if you want it in writing: chats handled, leads captured, reply time <1 second.

Worth 15 minutes? Reply "yes" — I'll build a free demo for YOUR business first.

— Vinayak, Abet Works ([+phone])
---

### Niche one-liners to swap into body ("Businesses like yours lose…")
- **Dental:** patients compare 3 clinics at 11pm — first price answer usually wins.
- **Restaurant:** party orders arrive on Instagram at midnight; nobody replies by morning.
- **Machine shop:** the customer is already importing when your email sits unanswered.

## 3. Instagram DM script (15/day — their profiles show they never reply to DMs)

**ID-4 breakup (day 7):** All the best with [+their handle] 🙏 — the demo link stays live: [DEMO LINK]

# Objection book — discovery script + every hard question answered

> Discovery goal is NEVER to convince on a call. It's: (1) diagnose the pain in their words, (2) leave the demo/experiment hook, (3) book the pilot with one measurable number.
> Call script first, then the objection library, then follow-up cadence.

---

## 1. The 20-minute discovery call (structure)

**0–3 min · Warm open**
"How are enquiries coming in these days — WhatsApp, calls, walk-ins? Biggest pain this month?"

**3–8 min · Quantify the pain (their own numbers)**
- "Roughly how many enquiries a day?"
- "What happens to them after staff hours?"
- "When a customer asks the price at 10pm, who answers? What does it cost you monthly in missed jobs, roughly?"
- Let THEM say the number. Write it down. That number is the pilot's before-metric.

**8–14 min · Present by proof, never by feature**
Read this sequence exactly:
1. "We do one thing: an AI front desk that answers your customers 24/7 — but it can only use what you approve in writing."
2. Open the demo link live: "Try to make it invent a discount. Go on." *(Refusal on the call = credibility spike.)*
3. "Every deployment also clears a 20-question adversarial retest before going live — SHIP or DO NOT SHIP. That's public too: [EVALREADY LINK]."
4. One sentence on delivery: "Live in 5 working days, week-1 report every Friday, care plan included."

**14–17 min · Pilot close (the only offer allowed on a call)**
- "Pilot: one week, live channel, one flat pilot fee of [₹ – placeholder, decide later]. One measurable number: your enquiries answered out-of-hours. Week-1 report decides everything after — no renewal pressure, no drama."
- Silence after the price. Take it.

**17–20 min · Confirm the next step in writing**
- WhatsApp them, while on the call, the sheet + demo + this sentence: "Pilot starts [date] — I need from you: your price list + services page. That's all."
- If hesitation: objection book below. No deals made on this call: follow-up cadence in §3.

## 2. The objection library (15 — reaction + proof asset to send)

| # | Objection | Your reply (mirror their number!) | Send after call |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Too expensive." | "Versus what — the [X] missed enquiries you quoted me? The pilot pays for itself if it catches even [2] of those." | one-pager PDF |
| 2 | "Let me think." | "Sure — take the demo link; the only thing I'll ask in our next call is: how many chats did it handle for the fake business. If it's boring numbers, I'm the wrong vendor." | demo + WA set time |
| 3 | "AI will say something wrong." | "It only knows what you sign. Out-of-document? It doesn't answer — it collects the contact. Try 5 minutes to break it: [DEMO]." | demo |
| 4 | "My nephew handles WhatsApp." | "He's free at 2pm — who does 2am? That's when people compare three clinics and pick who answered first." | demo |
| 5 | "I don't trust chatbots." | "Most people don't, and they're right — most bots invent things. Ours is the one that can't, and a public 20-test proves it: [EVALREADY]." | evalready |
| 6 | "WhatsApp business rules / bans?" | "Meta-approved template flows, business API where needed — the care plan keeps you compliant, no grey tricks." (If unsure: say you'll confirm in writing — never guess on compliance.) | keyword spec |
| 7 | "What if I want to cancel?" | "The pilot is one week, one report. Weak numbers = I tell you to stop. Strong numbers = you'll ask to extend." | dashboard link |
| 8 | "Copycat competitor bundled in." | "Your document is your moat: nobody copies signed prices + your voice in 5 days." | demo |
| 9 | "Not the right time." | "Fair. Which quarter's better — and shall I pencil a 10-min check-in then? (Calendar reality: organising happens in the enquiry, not in 'the right time'.)" | follow-up in 30 days |
| 10 | "Data safe?" | "Your leads stay yours in your sheet. Residency Mumbai, export anytime. DPDP-friendly posture; the knowledge document only contains what customers could hear anyway." | sheet demo |
| 11 | "Can it do [wild feature]?" | "Maybe — demo shows the front-desk core. Let's benchmark wild features AFTER a paid pilot that works." | demo |
| 12 | "We already have a chatbot" (other vendor) | "Great — run the same 20-test on it first: [EVALREADY]. If it SHIPs, keep it happily. If not, then we talk replacements." | evalready |
| 13 | "Send a proposal." (the forever email) | "Proposal = the one-pager attached. But the decision needs the demo, not the document — 10 minutes at [time]?" | one-pager PDF |
| 14 | "Discount, yaar." | *(prices not final — placeholder stance)* "Pilot pricing's already the floor. What I CAN do is smaller scope first, upgrade later — same money, less risk." | pilot plan |
| 15 | "Will it replace my staff?" | "It replaces the 7pm-11pm gap. Humans keep closes, surgeries, negotiations — that's the point of the design." | demo |

**Reading the room:** an objection with a question mark is interest; an objection with a period is a door closing — respect it, log the kill signal, move on.

## 3. Follow-up cadence (until yes or kill)

| Day | Move |
|---|---|
| 0 | Discovery/pitch — send links in-call |
| 2 | WhatsApp: one new number from THEIR industry (proof, not pressure) |
| 7 | Voice note 30s: "week-1 numbers from the pilot-run client" |
| 14 | Email: dashboard strip screenshot + one-line close |
| 30 | The breakup message (W-4) — door open |
| 60+ |0. Re-open only with a NEW proof (never "checking in") |

**Kill signals (stop chasing, log and leave):** no demo open by day 10 · "not now" ×2 · asks for price list before asking anything else (shopping signal) · ghosted after inviting to a scheduled call.

**Close math from the kit:** 20 touches → 1 discovery invite. 3 discovery calls → 1 pilot. A pilot converts multi-year via the care plan + dashboard.

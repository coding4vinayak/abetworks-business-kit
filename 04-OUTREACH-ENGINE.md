# BUSINESS 4 — OUTREACH ENGINE (the fuel line for everything)

**Role:** not a business you sell — the daily machine that fills Businesses 1 & 2 with clients. 60–75 minutes a day. Non-negotiable, even on full days. This is the engine that makes the rest live.

---

## Daily rhythm (60–75 min total)

| Min | Task |
|---|---|
| 0–20 | Follow-ups from days -1, -3, -7 (this is where 60% of closes happen) |
| 20–45 | 10 new cold messages (WhatsApp/IG/others) |
| 45–60 | 5 Upwork proposals OR 5 LinkedIn comments + 1 post |
| 60–75 | Update tracker: who, channel, got reply?, next follow-up date |

**Volume targets/week:** 100 messages · 25 proposals · 3–5 video demos sent. At this volume with good scripts: expect 5–15 replies, 2–5 demos/calls, 1–3 closes per week once warmed up.

---

## Where to harvest leads (fresh list every Sunday, 30 min)

- Google Maps: "[city] dental clinic / gym / restaurant / real estate / coaching / physiotherapy" → get phone/WhatsApp/IG
- Instagram/FB pages of local businesses (bad DM response = your lead)
- Fair/AliBaba sellers of hardware items, plus Etsy 3D printing shops (their poor product photos = your Fiverr 2D offer)
- LinkedIn: search "owner [niche] [city]" — for the higher-ticket AI+automation deals
- Job boards' small business hiring posts ("looking for part-time social media person" → automation proposal)

---

## Channel rules

- WhatsApp voice note > long text (the 45-sec voice note converts 2–3× better than paragraphs for local business owners who don't read fast)
- ONE message per day max per lead. Track it in sheet. Day -1, day -3, day -7 then stop and move to nurture.
- Sell the demo, not the bot. Every conversation → "I'll make a free 2-min demo of THIS for YOUR business" (10 min of your time after practice).

## Scripts (copy-paste, adjust name)

### 1. Cold open (local business, WhatsApp/IG)
> Hi [Name]! I saw [specific thing about their business — new menu / your new branch / your product on Instagram]. Quick question: when customers ask about timings/prices/booking in DMs, do you or your staff answer each one manually?
>
> I build AI assistants that answer those 24/7 and send you every serious customer's name + number. I actually made a free 2-min demo of how it'd look for YOUR business — want me to send it here?

### 2. The (voice note version, 45 sec)
> "Hi [Name], [you from X]. I'll be honest — I'll keep this super short. I help [their business type] stop losing customers in DMs at night. I made a free demo of an AI assistant for your business type — 2 minutes, no pressure, just to see if this is even something worth your attention. Should I send it as a video here or WhatsApp?"

### 3. Follow-up #2 (day 3)
> Hi [Name], following up — I actually went ahead and made a short 2-min demo showing how an AI assistant would answer YOUR customers based on the info on your page. Want me to send it? Zero obligation — if it's not for you, just say no and I'll stop.

### 4. Follow-up #3 (day 7) — value drop, then close-out
> Hi [Name] — last message from me, promise 🙂 I found 3 questions customers ask you most on your page that are being answered late or never: [1, 2, 3]. An AI assistant fixes exactly this for $X one-time. If budget's the issue I have a smaller starter option. No reply needed — door's open anytime.

### 5. Machine shop / print farm (CAD lane, LinkedIn/email)
> Hi [Name], mechanical engineer here — I do overflow CAD work for shops: sketches/old parts → clean 3D models + manufacturing drawings. Pay per part, no retainer, STEP/DXF/STL whatever fits your floor. Want 2 free samples of the kind of drawing you'd get? If capacity ever bites, I'm one message away.

### 6. E-commerce seller (product/render lane)
> Hi [Name], your [product] listing caught my eye — the photos are selling it less than they could. I do 3D renders from a few reference photos (engineer-grade, consistent angles, your brand colors). Want one free render of your best-seller to see the difference? If you like it, I'll price a pack for your catalog.

### 7. Objection: "too expensive" (AI bot or CAD)
> Fair — and for what it's worth, that's the average ask. Let me do the smaller version: [starter scope — 10 questions / 1 part]. That's [half price], one week, and you'll SEE the value. If it works, we grow from there. If not, honest feedback either way.

### 8. Objection: "AI bot might say something wrong"
> It only answers from the info YOU provide — prices, hours, policies. Anything outside that, it doesn't guess; it takes the customer's contact and forwards it to you. So no hallucinated price lists, ever.

### 9. Ask for referral (every delivery, every time, word for word)
> Quick favor — do you personally know 1 or 2 other [business type]s who have the same DM/phone-inquiry problem? If you send them my way, I'll give THEM the same starter offer I gave you, and you get [one month of maintenance free / a free extra feature] when any of them books. Takes a minute, saves me cold-calling strangers 🙂

### 10. Rejection save (when they say no now)
> No worries at all, thanks for being straightforward. One quick thing — is it "not the right time," or "not a priority"? No is totally fine either way — just asking so I know if I should check in next quarter or leave you be.

## Demo delivery playbook (the money moment)

1. 10-minute Botpress clone of their niche (clinic = ABC clinic flow; restaurant = table booking flow)
2. Record 2-min Loom: greet by THEIR name in intro sentence → demo 3 Q&As with THEIR actual menu/services → show the lead-capture landing in a sheet
3. End line: "This is demo-info entered in 4 minutes — imagine it with your real menu + prices. Setup = $X one-time, live in 5 days. If you like it, I can have YOURS live this week."

**Conversion math at current market:** demo → close rate runs 20–40% when the demo is personalized. 10 demos = 2–4 clients. This is why the engine exists.

---

## The value ladder (what you offer when a "no" is really a "bigger budget later")

Tier 1: FAQ bot, 10–15 questions ($30–60) → Tier 2: + booking + lead sheet ($100–200) → Tier 3: + monthly maintenance retainer ($20–50/mo) → Tier 4: + automations Tier (Gig 3 upsell — sheets/reports/reply drafting $140–300) → Tier 5: full AI ops partner ($300–800 setup + $100+/mo)

When someone can't afford Tier 2, offer Tier 1 WITHOUT shame. A $40 client today who LOVES the service becomes a $500 client in month 8. Small doors lead to big rooms.

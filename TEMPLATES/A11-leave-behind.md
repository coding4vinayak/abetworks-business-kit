## Print instructions (CUPS / any shop, 2 min)

1. Landscape **A4**, margins None, 2-up → duplicate slide → **A5 each — cut guides** 3mm bleed.
2. Paper: 300 gsm matte (shop knows as "card sheet"). Color print; b/w acceptable — demo QR still scans.
3. QR = free static QR to the `demo-front-desk.html` URL (any free generator; test-scan before the batch).
4. Lamination: rounded corners only — glossy costs extra, skip.

## Where these cards go (distribution discipline)

- In the folder: 20 always, refilled weekly — never hand the LAST one to a manager (signal of scarcity).
- After each visit: log in `trackers/outreach-tracker.csv` with channel=`card`, and within 48h the W-2 follow-up referencing "the card" ("did the QR trick you?" is a better opener than any pitch).

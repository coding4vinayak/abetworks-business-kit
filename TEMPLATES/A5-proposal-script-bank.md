# TEMPLATE A5 — PROPOSAL & SCRIPT BANK (by niche — pick, personalize, send)

**Rule of all cold messages:** ONE specific observation about THEIR business + the problem they feel daily + ask a small question. Never open with "I offer services." Personalized > perfect.

---

## Part 1 — Upwork proposals (5 variants)

### P1 — Chatbot job
```
Hi [Name],

You mentioned customers ask [exact thing from their job post] — that's usually the #1 time-cost for [store type]. I build AI assistants that answer from the seller's own data (prices, policies, catalogs) with a strict rule: anything not in the data, the bot collects contact info instead of guessing.

I built a similar assistant for [restaurant/clinic] — 2-min demo: [LOOM LINK].

Three quick questions to scope it right:
1. Where should captured leads land (email / Sheet / CRM)?
2. How many core Q&A items do you expect?
3. Website platform — WordPress, Shopify, or custom?

I can deliver the first demo build within [3 days].

[YOUR NAME] — Mechanical engineer turned AI-automation builder; I test everything twice before delivery.
```

```
[YOUR NAME]
```

```
Hi [Name],

Job: [their task — e.g. STL from a sketch]. Mechanical engineer here ([year]s of CAD practice: Fusion 360 / Onshape; exports STEP, IGES, STL, DXF).

My standard for this: dimensions obey your drawing; missing dims get solved engineering-style (standard fits) AND flagged to you in writing — nothing silently guessed.
```

*Fill the observation from 60 seconds on their page. That one sentence is the whole trick — it proves you didn't blast 500 people.*

## Part 3 — Follow-up discipline (mirrors 04)

- Day 0 open → Day 3 (value/demo attached) → Day 7 (close-out). Archive after.
- Track EVERYTHING in the tracker (05) — niches that don't reply at all get dropped after 30 contacts, not 300.

## Part 4 — Upwork profile extras (fill-once)

- **Portfolio titles:** "AI Front Desk — [niche] demo" ×3, "Photo-to-model CAD conversion", "GD&T drawing sheet", "Before/after: product renders"
- **Project catalog pricing:** chatbot starter $50 / full setup $150 / CAD conversion $30 — mirrors your Fiverr ladder
- **Fill availability NOW:** 25–40 hrs — real, adjusted later; never claim 60.

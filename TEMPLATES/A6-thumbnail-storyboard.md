# TEMPLATE A6 — GIG THUMBNAIL STORYBOARDS (exact Canva instructions)

Canvas: **1280 × 769 px** (Fiverr standard). No design skills needed — clone these layouts. Rule: it must be readable at 300×180px (that's the size buyers first see in search — TEST by zooming out 25%).

---

## COLOR RECIPE (all thumbnails, all platforms)

- Background: solid deep navy `#0F2137` or soft cream `#FFF7EE` — never grayscale-only, never cluttered
- Accent 1 (headline): white
- Accent 2 (support block / CTA strip): vivid orange `#FF6B35` or trust-blue `#2E86FF`
- Font: **Montserrat ExtraBold / Poppins Bold** (free in Canva) — one font family per image, max 2 sizes
- Text on busy photo? add a navy rectangle at 80% transparency behind text. Always.

## Make-it-once rules

1. Make all 6 in one 2-hr Canva session (template duplication, swap elements)
2. Duplicate each → flip to Variant B (swap navy↔cream, headline word order). Fiverr lets 3 images min — test A after 500 impressions, swap if <2% click-through
3. Free stock texture packs in Canva ("paper", "gradient mesh") beat paid stock — accent color only where the eye should land
4. Order of gig images: (1) hook thumb (2) demo screenshot collage (3) before/after (4) your dashboard (5) process timeline (6) testimonial/empty-placeholder until reviews exist
5. Add your tiny initials-corner watermark bottom-right 12px — screenshots won't hurt you anymore

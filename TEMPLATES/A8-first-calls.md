# TEMPLATE A8 — FIRST CALLS PLAYBOOK (voice calls close what messages start)

**Context:** your texts (04/A5) get replies. Replies say "can you call?" — these 2 call types make the sale. WhatsApp voice is default for local owners (free, no app install). Keep every call under 10 minutes.

---

## BEFORE you call anyone

- Have on screen: outreach tracker row for them (their page, their business type, what they said in texts)
- 2-min Loom demo ALREADY made for them (or make it while on call — "give me 5 minutes, I'll send it right here")
- Water, door closed, script visible. Smile out loud — they can hear posture.
- Note: if THEY call you from an Upwork inquiry: P5 script + schedule call, aim for the demo call directly.

---

## CALL 1 — Discovery (8–10 min, the qualification call)

**Goal:** learn their DM pain + get permission to send the demo. NOT to sell the price.

| Min | Step (exact words) |
|---|---|
| 0–1 | "Hi [Name], [your name] — we messaged about the AI assistant for [Business]. Is this a good 5 minutes?" (**if no: "Perfect — evening better?"** and set the time NOW) |
| 1–3 | Warm-up (90 sec max): "How long have you been running [Business]?" — listen, note one fact you'll reuse |
| 3–6 | The 3 pain questions (THE call): ①"About customers messaging — who answers today, you or staff?" ②"Roughly how many questions a day?" ③"What happens when nobody answers at night?" (…silence does the selling) |
| 6–8 | Bridge: "So here's what I'm building for someone like you: an assistant on your page answering [prices/hours/booking] 24/7 and saving every serious buyer's number. I already made a 2-min demo on YOUR info — **send it here or WhatsApp after lunch?**" |
| 8–9 | Micro-close: "When you see the demo, if it makes sense, is next week a fair time to get it live?" (yes/no behavior predictor — if YES, they're yours; if hesitant, park it in tracker "nurture") |
| Close-out | "I'll WhatsApp the demo by [time] — talk tomorrow?" |

**Log it instantly:** pain facts + their exact words ("we lose so many customers at night, honestly") — their words become YOUR ad copy later.

## CALL 2 — Demo + close (7 min, THE money call)

**Opening (30 sec):** "Ready? Open your [website/page] on the phone — I'll call the link to you." Send shareable link. Watch their bot load WITH them (loss-aversion: they watch their own customers' experience, not your pitch).

**The 4-beat demo (3.5 min):**
1. Their #1 question answered instantly (from intake texts)
2. Booking flow live — "type like a customer would"; let THEM hold the phone
3. Leads landing in the Sheet/table — "that's [name] captured at [time]"
4. The zero-hallucination beat: ask it an UNLISTED question → watch it capture contact instead of inventing → "It doesn't make up prices. That's why staff stay trusted."

**Silence skill:** after the 2am beat, count 3 seconds silently. Whoever talks first decides the price — make it them: "What did you think?"

**The close (60 sec, assumptive):**
> "So — full setup live in 5 days: your info loaded, on your site, lead capture, and I handle everything. $[X] one-time, half now to start, and 1 month of support free. Want it live before [next weekend / festival / your busiest season]?"

**Payment line:** "I'll send the payment request/agreement right now — 5 minutes, and I start Part 1 today." (A4 contract + 50/50)

## Objection micro-answers mid-call (from 08, compressed for voice)

- Expensive → "Fair. The smaller starter is $[X]: top-10 questions only, 1 week — you'll SEE the value." (never discount same scope)
- "I have a guy who said it's free" → "Take the free option — most likely it breaks within two weeks and it'll cost you customers. Mine comes with 30-day support and a live demo first."
- "Think about it" → "Of course — what's the part you're not sure about: the price, the trust, or the timing?" (name it for them)
- "Does it handle [crazy scenario]?" → Honest: "I've never tested that exact case. Let me test and answer by tomorrow." (honesty > instant)

## If THEY call YOU (inbound — rare at first, then normal)

- Fast discovery (2 min only): volume? who answers? site/link?
- "Let me build the demo today and WhatsApp you by evening — I'll call at [time]." Inbound + demo-sameday = highest close-rate combo you will ever have. Protect response speed <1hr always.

## Voice drills (Saturday 10 min, out loud, local language)

1. Say your offer ONE sentence without taking a breath-sip: "I build AI front desks for [type] that answer customers 24/7 and save every serious buyer's number — live in 5 days, done-for-you."
2. Practice the pain question ①②③ mixed 3× each
3. Practice the 3-second silence after demos (count fingers in your lap; don't fill the silence with features)

## The 5 call mistakes that kill deals (skim monthly)

1. Pitching in minute 1 instead of asking the 3 pain questions
2. Explaining "how it works technically" — owners buy OUTCOMES ("no missed buyers at 2am"), not architecture
3. Filling every silence with more talking (silence = their wallet opening)
4. No next-step time set before hanging up → call ends "we'll be in touch" = archived, savvy
5. Calling from a noisy market street — schedule or message first, your voice is a product too
